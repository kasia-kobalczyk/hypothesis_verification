You are checking whether cited scientific records address the same construct as a
proposition. You are NOT judging whether the proposition is true, and you are NOT
judging whether the records are good science.

A literature assessor cited the records below as bearing on the proposition, and
quoted the spans shown. Decide whether what those records actually measure, test or
report is the construct the proposition asserts.

Use exactly one label:

- "direct": the records measure, test or report the proposition's construct as
  stated: the same system, the same relation or quantity, and the same condition,
  or a construct that is scientifically equivalent without any further inference.
- "partial": the records address the proposition's construct only in part. For
  example, the right relation in a different condition or system, or one component
  of a claim that has several.
- "mismatch": the records address a related but different construct. The step from
  what they report to what the proposition asserts is itself an inference the records
  do not make.

Do not bridge the gap yourself. If connecting the records to the proposition needs
reasoning the records do not report, the answer is not "direct".

Distinctions that are easy to miss:
- a correlate, marker or representation of something is not its causal role;
- where or how often something occurs is not how or when it originated;
- a property of a general class is not a property of one specific member;
- one component of a compound claim is not the whole claim;
- evidence that a mechanism can operate is not evidence that it does operate in the
  specific setting the proposition names.

PROPOSITION:
$proposition

SPANS QUOTED BY THE ASSESSOR:
$spans

CITED RECORDS:
$records

Return a single JSON object, and nothing else:

{
  "construct_match": "<direct|partial|mismatch>",
  "proposition_construct": "<what the proposition asserts, in one short phrase>",
  "evidence_construct": "<what the records actually measure, test or report, in one short phrase>",
  "gap": "<what is missing or different; empty string if direct>",
  "rationale": "<one or two sentences>"
}
