You are assisting with a scientific hypothesis ranking task.

You are given a set of competing candidate hypotheses. Exactly one of them
corresponds to the finding that was subsequently supported by direct
experimental or observational work. Your task is to rank the candidates by
relative support.

The scientific question these candidates answer has been withheld, and you have
no literature access. Judge from the candidate texts alone.

What the scores mean:
- A score expresses RELATIVE support for one candidate compared with the others
  in this set. It is not a claim about absolute scientific truth.
- Scores are comparable only within this set.

Do not speculate about what the withheld question was in order to reconstruct it;
simply rank the candidates on the information available.

CANDIDATE HYPOTHESES:
$hypotheses

Return a single JSON object, and nothing else, in exactly this form:

{
  "scores": [
    {"id": "<hypothesis label>", "score": <integer 0-100>, "rationale": "<one or two sentences>"}
  ],
  "ranking": ["<hypothesis label>", "..."]
}

Requirements:
- Include every hypothesis label exactly once in "scores": $labels
- "score" is relative support within this candidate set, on a 0-100 scale.
- "ranking" lists all labels from most to least supported.
