You are deciding whether one scientific record bears DIRECTLY on one proposition.

Decide first, then justify.

THE ONLY QUESTION

Does this record report, measure, test, or state the proposition below, or its
negation?

"Directly" is about THIS proposition, not about any larger hypothesis. The
proposition may itself sit several reasoning steps away from whatever question
produced it. That is irrelevant to you.

A record that reports something from which the proposition could be INFERRED — via
a mechanism, an adjacent system, an upstream cause, an analogous molecule — does
NOT bear directly on it. Answer NO_DIRECT_EVIDENCE.

Inferential steps between claims are recorded and judged elsewhere in this system.
If you bridge from a finding to the proposition using scientific knowledge of your
own, you are doing that work in the wrong place, where nobody can check or weight
it. Report what is stated, not what follows.

GROUNDING

If you answer anything other than NO_DIRECT_EVIDENCE you must quote a
`supporting_span`: a contiguous span copied EXACTLY, character for character, from
the abstract below. It is checked automatically. A paraphrase fails.

If completing the connection needs a fact that is in neither the record nor the
proposition, set "requires_external_bridge" to true and answer
NO_DIRECT_EVIDENCE.

PROPOSITION:
$proposition

RECORD:
$record

Return a single JSON object, and nothing else:

{
  "category": "<DIRECT_EVIDENCE or NO_DIRECT_EVIDENCE>",
  "direction": "<supports, contradicts, or none>",
  "strength": "<strong, moderate, weak, or null>",
  "supporting_span": "<exact quote from the abstract; null otherwise>",
  "requires_external_bridge": false,
  "would_need_inference": false,
  "proposition_unassessable": false,
  "rationale": "<1-3 sentences describing what the record states>"
}

Set "would_need_inference" to true when the record is about the right subject and
could support the proposition only through a reasoning step. That case is
NO_DIRECT_EVIDENCE, and the flag records that a link may exist for the graph to
represent.
