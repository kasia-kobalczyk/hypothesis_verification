You are assisting with a scientific hypothesis ranking task.

You are given a scientific question and a set of competing candidate hypotheses.
Exactly one of them corresponds to the finding that was subsequently supported by
direct experimental or observational work. Your task is to rank the candidates by
how likely each one is to be that finding.

You have no literature access for this task. Judge using your scientific
reasoning about the question and the candidates alone.

Guidance:
- Judge the scientific substance of each candidate, not its length, formatting,
  confidence of tone, or level of technical detail.
- A candidate that is more specific is not thereby more likely to be correct.
- Consider mechanistic plausibility, consistency with established science, and
  whether the claimed effect direction and magnitude are reasonable.
- Do not assume the candidates are ordered in any meaningful way.

QUESTION:
$question

CANDIDATE HYPOTHESES:
$hypotheses

Return a single JSON object, and nothing else, in exactly this form:

{
  "scores": [
    {"id": "<hypothesis label>", "score": <integer 0-100>, "rationale": "<one or two sentences>"}
  ],
  "ranking": ["<hypothesis label>", "..."]
}

Requirements:
- Include every hypothesis label exactly once in "scores": $labels
- "score" is your probability, on a 0-100 scale, that this candidate is the
  hypothesis that was subsequently supported. Scores need not sum to 100.
- "ranking" lists all labels from most to least likely.
