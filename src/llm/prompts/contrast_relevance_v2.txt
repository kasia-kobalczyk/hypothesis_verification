proposition that distinguishes competing explanations. You are NOT judging whether the
proposition is true, and NOT judging the quality of the science.

PROPOSITION:
$proposition

ITS CONTRAST-BEARING ELEMENT:
$element

SPANS QUOTED BY THE ASSESSOR WHO CITED THE RECORDS:
$spans

CITED RECORDS:
$records

Answer three questions about the records themselves. Do not bridge gaps with your own
reasoning: if a step is not reported in the records, it is not there.

Q1. Do the records measure, test or directly report the CONTRAST VARIABLE (its value,
    ordering or state), in a system and condition that matches the element?

    setting (for example a reconstituted, simplified, model, engineered or different

    answer "partly", not "yes".

Q2. Do the records support the SHARED CONTEXT (background every candidate accepts)?
    "yes" or "no".

Q3. Do the records concern a DIFFERENT construct or measurement that could be mistaken for
    the contrast variable (a correlate, a marker, a possibility rather than an occurrence,
    a property of a wider class, a different system)? "yes" or "no".
