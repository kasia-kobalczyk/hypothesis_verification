You are judging how strongly each of several competing hypotheses implies one
scientific proposition.

For every candidate below, answer: if that candidate were true, how likely would
the proposition be?

Use exactly one of these ordinal labels per candidate:
$labels

Guidance:
- "strongly_implied" means the candidate would make the proposition nearly
  certain; "implied" means clearly expected; "weakly_implied" means somewhat
  more likely than otherwise.
- "neutral" means the candidate says nothing either way about the proposition.
  Use it freely — most propositions are irrelevant to most candidates, and a
  false implication is worse than an honest "neutral".
- "unlikely" and "strongly_contradicted" mean the candidate makes the
  proposition less likely, or nearly rules it out.
- Judge the scientific relationship only. You have no literature access, and you
  are NOT being asked whether the proposition is actually true — only what each
  candidate would predict.
- Do not reward a candidate for being longer, more detailed or more confident.

PROPOSITION:
$proposition

CANDIDATES:
$candidates
